Five risk levels, clearly marked

Every clause gets a severity rating. Nothing is hidden or softened.

Critical risk

Clauses that expose you to major liability, unlimited damages, rights forfeiture, or legal jeopardy. Requires immediate attention before signing.

Caution

Unusual terms that disadvantage you but may be negotiable. Worth pushing back on before signing.

Ambiguous

Vague language, undefined terms, or unclear jurisdiction — the company can interpret these however it wants later.

Safe

Standard, fair clauses with no unusual scope. Included for completeness.

Neutral

Boilerplate language with no notable risk. Nothing to act on.
